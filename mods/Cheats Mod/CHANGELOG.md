# Changelog


- No Bullying Policy compatibility is now fully optional and detected at runtime by reflection, including verification that its Harmony patch is active. Cheats Mod has no compile-time reference to or dependency on No Bullying Policy, and its warning/preview text appears only when that mod is enabled and its No Bullying Policy option is selected. Player-facing text now names **No Bullying Policy** explicitly.
- Corrected **Adjust bullying**'s effects preview around dating-member ejections. It now says the clique can remain bullying after the ejection, shows only effects that actually occur, and lists the ongoing -10 Mental Stamina weekly effect once per clique while that clique remains bullying.
- Simplified the **Adjust bullying** player-facing preview text. It now states the actual outcome directly (start/stop bullying, ejection, bond and Mental Stamina changes, stopped/resumed members, and No Bullying behavior) without exposing internal method names or implementation terminology.
- **Adjust bullying** now preserves the full vanilla `AddBulliedGirl` consequences. A clique member dating the victim is violently ejected before any stopped-bullying exception can apply, including the vanilla relationship and Mental Stamina effects; the cheat no longer shields selected stopped members from those consequences. The right-side summary now previews pending effects before confirmation, including starts/stops, exact bond percentage changes and negative trends, dating-driven violent ejections, Mental Stamina changes, leader replacement/disband outcomes, stopped/resumed participation, and the temporary No Bullying Policy bypass.
- Fixed **Adjust bullying** against the sibling **No Bullying Policy** mod. The provided regression save has the no-bullying value selected, whose Harmony prefix blocks vanilla `AddBulliedGirl`; the cheat temporarily exposes that policy's default value only around the explicit native call and restores the player's policy immediately afterward. It warns that the still-enabled policy may clear forced bullying on the next weekly update.
- Tightened the shared clique cards used by **Adjust bullying** and **Adjust cliques**. Compact card height now follows the actual number of member rows instead of reserving a fixed 450-pixel block, idol cards are inset farther from the left edge, member cards sit lower beneath the **Members** heading, and the leader/member vertical spacing is reduced without overlapping the native idol cards.

- Converted **Add friends**, **Add best friends**, **Add disliked idols**, and **Add hated idols** into true **Adjust** cheats. Existing relationships in the selected category are preselected, card clicks can add/remove that category, and each destination idol now has a pencil editor for the exact bond value and the game’s positive/neutral/negative dynamic trend. Bond edits are encoded into the vanilla saved `Vals` history; vanilla does not serialize `Dynamic`, so the UI explicitly notes that trend resets to Neutral after loading a save.
- Converted **Add bullies** into **Adjust bullying**. Existing bullying cliques are preselected and may be deselected to stop bullying, newly selected cliques use vanilla `AddBulliedGirl`, and the third stage preloads and edits the persisted stopped-bullying exceptions while still allowing none. Old public `OpenAdd*` entry points remain as compatibility aliases.
- Fixed the shared clique-card layout used by **Adjust bullying** and **Adjust cliques**: idol cards now sit farther right and farther below the **Leader**/**Members** headings, and compact cards use a two-column member layout to avoid native portrait/text bleed clipping at the scroll edge.
- Added/updated all required button labels, tooltips, popup text, editor text, and notifications in all seven supported languages, and repaired the previously corrupted literal question-mark strings in the cumulative localization files.

- Added **Add hated idols to idol** as a dedicated relationship cheat.
- Reworked **Add bullies to idol** into three stages: victim, one-or-more existing clique selection, then optional selection of clique members who have stopped bullying. Leaving the third stage empty keeps the entire selected clique(s) bullying. The cheat now calls the game’s native `AddBulliedGirl` operation before applying persisted stopped-bullying members.
- Upgraded clique browsing in both **Adjust cliques** and **Add bullies** to portrait-rich cards with separate **Leader** and **Members** sections.
- Repaired the cumulative relationship/clique/bullying localization additions in all seven supported languages, including non-Latin strings that had been corrupted to question marks in the interim package.

- Added **Add friends to idol**, **Add best friends to idol**, and **Add disliked idols to idol**. Each uses a two-step target/multi-select picker with Back and Cancel controls; existing best-friend and hate relationships are not downgraded by the weaker corresponding action.
- Added **Adjust cliques**, listing existing cliques and allowing safe membership edits, peaceful or the game's native hostile/violent removal behavior, disbanding with confirmation, and creation of new 1–5 member cliques. Idols already in another clique must be removed there first.
- Added button labels, tooltips, popup instructions, controls, and notifications for these cheats in all seven supported languages.

- Numeric entry dialogs now use a fully opaque background, including graduation date, fame, and stat editors.
- Updated the EroEvents cheats menu with the shared native buttons, game fonts, search field, and scrolling controls. Both cheat search fields display “Search” in English and its translation in the other supported languages.
- Unique-idol recruitment cards now have native pencil buttons for names, ages, and all eight stats. Changes apply to the recruitment preview and carry into the hired idol; scrolling retains edits. Ages are restricted to the lower of the original age or 16 through 120, and stats to 0–100. Already-recruited idols cannot be edited from the recruitment screen.
- Added opaque name-entry dialogs and translated all new editor labels and validation messages into the seven supported languages.

- The bully picker now lists every selected idol by name below the selection count, with a scrollable list for large selections and a translated empty-selection message.
- Replaced the selector and numeric dialog action buttons with the same native Settings button templates used by Mod Buttons, preserving their gradient, shadow, hover behavior, and readable white labels. Button language bindings retain the cheat captions when the popup opens.

- Reworked the graduation, selected-idol, and unique-idol recruitment selectors with embedded IM UI Framework controls, rounded game surfaces, native scroll indicators, game fonts, and canvas-aware sizing. The framework does not require a separate Workshop installation.
- Replaced the selected-idol "stats 100" action with eight independent 0–100 controls. Each has MIN/MAX, native salary minus/plus/pencil buttons, and bounded keyboard entry. Edits are staged until Apply; switching idols or closing discards unapplied changes.
- Fame now uses the same controls with whole-number limits of 1–10. Graduation date controls support native minus/plus buttons and keyboard entry for day, month, and year, while retaining the minimum three-month delay.
- Cancelling an announced graduation now applies the replacement date chosen in the same screen and clears the pending retirement dialogue.
- Preserved targeted scandal, non-producer breakup, and producer-relationship actions, and recruitment search, portrait choices, lazy portrait loading, and duplicate-recruitment prevention.
- Localized the changed labels, instructions, numeric input validation, and notifications in all seven supported languages; dates and numbers follow the selected game language.

- Added **Complete all room activities**, which finishes each currently running finite room timer once through the game's normal completion handler. Training, project production, auditions, proposals, loans, dates, treatment, and timed room scenes retain their normal outcomes and result prompts.
- Added **Add bullies to idol** using the existing idol picker: choose a victim and press **Next**, select multiple bullies with green selection highlights, then press **OK** to apply. Existing bullies remain unchanged; the victim and already-active bullies are excluded from the second picker.
- Bullying uses the game's saved clique records and per-member exclusions, preserving existing clique memberships and avoiding accidental recruitment of unselected members.
- Added both cheats' button text, tooltips, picker labels, instructions, and notifications in English, Simplified Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Russian.
