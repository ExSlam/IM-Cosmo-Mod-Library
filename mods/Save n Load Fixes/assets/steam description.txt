Save n Load Fixes repairs save/load bugs and related gameplay problems in Idol Manager. It helps your agency keep its progress, relationships, schedules and financial records when you save and return to the game.

[olist]
[*][b]Saving and loading:[/b] Prevents overlapping saves from finishing in the wrong order and loading from reading an unfinished save.
[*][b]Save and Exit:[/b] Waits for SNLF and participating mods to finish their save attempts before exiting or returning to the main menu, including attempts that fail.
[*][b]Save notifications:[/b] Shows when saving starts, finishes or fails.
[*][b]Money and large totals:[/b] Fixes calculations and displays that could turn large amounts into incorrect or negative numbers, including salaries, loans, business income, theater revenue and fan totals.
[*][b]Training and business:[/b] Preserves training progress, paused idols, remaining job time and business proposals selected by events.
[*][b]Singles, shows, concerts, tours and elections:[/b] Restores missing project progress information and fixes scheduling and completion-date errors.
[*][b]Fans and target audiences:[/b] Preserves show fan appeal, single comparisons and group audience settings after loading.
[*][b]Idol relationships:[/b] Preserves relationship behavior across saves and properly clears both idols' relationship state when you force them to break up.
[*][b]Graduation:[/b] Fixes graduation-date changes that were not being applied and restores pending successor introductions after loading.
[*][b]Activities and idol pushes:[/b] Preserves queued activities and recent activity history, and resets push duration when you change the selected idol.
[*][b]Story events and agency scenes:[/b] Preserves event timing, queued dialogue setup and pending scenes, and fixes events that remained active after ending.
[*][b]Awards:[/b] Preserves pending nominations and fixes inconsistent nominated singles and award-speech choices.
[*][b]Tutorials:[/b] Restores missing tutorial progress and delayed steps, and clears effects left behind by finished or skipped tutorials.
[*][b]SNS:[/b] Preserves saved messages and their replies after loading.
[*][b]Audition portraits:[/b] Retries portraits that fail to load and replaces candidates whose portraits still cannot be displayed.
[*][b]Modded idol and staff portraits:[/b] Preserves original portrait choices when a portrait pack is temporarily unavailable.
[*][b]Older saves:[/b] Keeps reconstructed idol ages, fan distribution, relationships and rival groups consistent across repeated loads.
[*][b]Reloading during play:[/b] Stops delayed actions from a previous load from affecting your current game.
[*][b]Settings and other gameplay fixes:[/b] Fixes saved settings not applying at startup, along with issues involving birthday checks, bankruptcy warnings, risky marketing and graduation records.
[/olist]
