[h1]Staff Portraits[/h1]

Give ordinary agency staff customizable composite portraits from a dedicated staff-only portrait pool.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Automatically gives eligible ordinary staff a randomized composite portrait when a valid staff portrait pack is installed.
[*]Adds [b]Staff Stylist[/b] to the Mod Buttons Action Hub.
[*]Choose the staff member, portrait pack, outfit/body, hair, face, and optional accessory.
[*]Preview changes, randomize a combination, then apply it.
[*]Keeps staff portrait assets separate from the idol portrait asset pool.
[*]Preserves staff portrait selections through the game's existing staff save data using stable pack and asset IDs.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Mod Buttons[/b] is required to open Staff Stylist.
[*]At least one enabled staff portrait pack containing [code]StaffPortraits/pack.json[/code] is required for new portraits.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility behavior[/h1]
Staff Portraits does not automatically replace the player/producer, former idols who became staff, unique named staff, or staff who already have composite portrait data.

The base mod does not borrow idol artwork. If no staff portrait pack is installed, existing staff portraits remain unchanged and Staff Stylist tells you that a pack is required.

Keep any portrait pack used by a save enabled. If a required pack is unavailable when you load a save, restore the pack and reload before saving again.
