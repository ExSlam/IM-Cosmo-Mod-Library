[h1]Staff Portraits[/h1]

Give ordinary agency staff customizable composite portraits from dedicated staff packs or compatible loaded Unique Idol packs.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Automatically gives eligible ordinary staff a randomized composite portrait when a valid dedicated staff portrait pack is installed.
[*]Adds [b]Staff Stylist[/b] to the Mod Buttons Action Hub.
[*]Switch between [b]Staff Packs[/b] and [b]Unique Idol Packs[/b] while styling a staff member.
[*]Choose the pack, outfit/body, hair, face, and optional accessory, then preview, randomize, and apply.
[*]Unique Idol mode reuses compatible artwork that Idol Manager already loaded; it does not scrape or duplicate the source files.
[*]Dedicated staff assets keep Staff Portraits namespaced save IDs, while Unique Idol selections keep the source mod's original IDs.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Mod Buttons[/b] is required to open Staff Stylist.
[*]Automatic staff portraits require an enabled dedicated pack containing [code]StaffPortraits/pack.json[/code].
[*]Manual Unique Idol styling requires an enabled compatible unique-idol mod with complete 1024 x 1500 body, hair, and face assets.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility behavior[/h1]
Staff Portraits does not automatically replace the player/producer, former idols who became staff, unique named staff, or staff who already have composite portrait data.

Unique Idol artwork is [b]manual-only[/b]: installing a character pack will not make new hires randomly become that character. Choose Unique Idol Packs in Staff Stylist when you want to use one deliberately.

Keep every Staff Pack or Unique Idol source used by a save enabled so its artwork and IDs remain available.
