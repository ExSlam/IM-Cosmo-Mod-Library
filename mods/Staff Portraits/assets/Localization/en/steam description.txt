[h1]Staff Portraits[/h1]

Give ordinary agency staff customizable composite portraits from a dedicated staff-only portrait pool.

[b]Author: Cosmo[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Random staff portraits when a valid staff portrait pack is installed.
[*]Staff Stylist in the Mod Buttons Action Hub.
[*]Choose portrait pack, outfit/body, hair, face, and optional accessory.
[*]Preview, randomize, and apply changes.
[*]Staff portrait assets remain separate from the idol portrait pool.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*]Mod Buttons is required to open Staff Stylist.
[*]A staff portrait pack containing StaffPortraits/pack.json is required for new portraits.
[/list]

Former idols, unique named staff, and staff who already have composite portraits are not automatically replaced. The base mod does not borrow idol artwork. Keep portrait packs used by a save enabled so their artwork remains available.
