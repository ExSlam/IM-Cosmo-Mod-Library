[h1]Staff Portraits[/h1]

Give ordinary agency staff customizable composite portraits from dedicated staff packs or compatible loaded Unique Idol packs.

[b]Author: Cosmo[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Random automatic portraits from dedicated Staff Packs.
[*]Staff Stylist in the Mod Buttons Action Hub.
[*]Switch between Staff Packs and Unique Idol Packs.
[*]Choose pack, outfit/body, hair, face, and optional accessory, then preview, randomize, and apply.
[*]Unique Idol mode reuses compatible artwork already loaded by Idol Manager instead of copying it.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*]Mod Buttons is required to open Staff Stylist.
[*]Automatic portraits require a dedicated pack containing StaffPortraits/pack.json.
[*]Manual Unique Idol styling requires an enabled compatible unique-idol mod with complete 1024 x 1500 body, hair, and face assets.
[/list]

The player/producer, former idols who became staff, unique named staff, and staff with existing composite portraits are not automatically replaced. Unique Idol artwork is manual-only and is never used for automatic hiring/backfill assignments. Keep every Staff Pack or Unique Idol source used by a save enabled.
